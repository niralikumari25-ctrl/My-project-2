LAKME Redesign

This project is a modern redesign of the Lakme company’s website using HTML, CSS, and a touch of JavaScript for interactivity. The goal was to improve user experience and make the design more attractive and responsive.

Quality

Lakme lipsticks are known for their good quality, rich pigmentation, and smooth texture. They offer long-lasting color, moisturizing formula, and a wide range of shades suitable for all skin tones. Most Lakme lipsticks have a creamy or matte finish and glide easily on the lips without drying them out. The brand is also appreciated for being affordable and easily available in India.
